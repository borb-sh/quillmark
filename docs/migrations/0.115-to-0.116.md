# 0.115 → 0.116 — a card no kind claims, and a body under `body.enabled: false`, render and warn

The break nothing reports: **a document that failed to render now renders.**
A card with no `$kind`, a card whose `$kind` the quill does not declare, and
body prose under `body.enabled: false` were fatal `validation::*` errors. Each
is now a `Warning` from `quill.validate(doc)`, and the render proceeds without
the input. A pipeline that relied on the render failing to catch these
documents catches nothing: run `quill.validate(doc)` and act on the three
codes below.

The rule behind it: a render fails only where the engine would have to invent
what the author wrote. Input no declaration claims displaces nothing, so it
renders and warns
([SCHEMAS.md § What blocks a render](https://github.com/borb-sh/quillmark/blob/main/prose/canon/SCHEMAS.md#what-blocks-a-render)).

| Break | Surface | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A card no declared kind claims renders instead of failing | rendered output | Run `quill.validate(doc)`; treat `validation::unknown_card` and `validation::kindless_card` as blocking where your pipeline needs them to be |
| Body prose under `body.enabled: false` renders without the body instead of failing | rendered output | Same, for `validation::body_disabled` |
| A card with no `$kind` is `validation::kindless_card`, not `validation::unknown_card` with `card: ""` | diagnostics | Route on the new code. Its `args` carry `allowed`, the quill's declared kinds |
| `validation::unknown_card` gains `allowed`, the quill's declared kinds | diagnostics | None; the key is additive |
| `validation::unknown_card`, `kindless_card` and `body_disabled` are `Severity::Warning` | diagnostics | A consumer treating every `validation::*` code as fatal routes on severity instead |
| `ValidationError::UnknownCard` and `ValidationError::BodyDisabled` are deleted | Rust | An exhaustive `match` loses two arms. `QuillConfig::validate_document` returns only what fails a render; `Quill::validate` carries the warnings |

## What reaches the plate

A card no kind claims keeps its place in `$cards`, fields verbatim and
uncoerced, with its `$kind` if it names one and no `$body`. A plate that reads
the discriminator with `card.at("$kind", default: none)` and branches on the
kinds it knows falls through on it, which is what every fixture plate does. A
plate reading `card.at("$kind")` bare fails to compile on a kindless card:
read it with a default.

A body under `body.enabled: false` does not reach the plate, as before.
