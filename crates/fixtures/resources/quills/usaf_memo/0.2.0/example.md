Airfield driving certification has run on three separate test banks since the
2024 group realignment. Pass rates are therefore not comparable across groups,
and a driver certified under one bank cannot be credited under another. This
memorandum directs a single bank and sets the transition date.

Effective 1 May 2026, all airfield driving certification will use the
consolidated test bank at Attachment 1. Unit airfield driving representatives
will:

- Retire local test banks and archive them for one year.
- Recertify any driver whose certificate predates 1 January 2026.
- Report recertification totals to 60 OSS/OSA by the fifth duty day monthly.

Certificates issued before 1 May 2026 remain valid until the expiration printed
on them. No driver loses flight line access solely as a result of this change.

Direct questions to the Airfield Management Office, DSN 837-2255.
